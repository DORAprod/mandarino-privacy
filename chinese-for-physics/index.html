Privacy Policy

Mandarino Chinese for Physics (Android)

Last updated: November 6, 2026
App package: com.mandarino.chineseforphysics
Privacy contact: mandarinohsk@tiscali.it

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application Mandarino Chinese for Physics handles information when you use its educational content, speech features, and optional in-app purchases.

1. Information handled by the app

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to use its educational features. The app stores certain information locally on your device so it can remember your preferences and access to purchased courses, including:

  • Selected interface language, Pinyin display preferences, and speech playback speed.
  • Identifiers of Google Play products that the app recognizes as purchased or redeemed, used to enable the corresponding course content.

The current app version does not integrate a third-party advertising SDK or a standalone user-behavior analytics service.

2. Google Play purchases and promotional codes

Optional course purchases and promotional-code redemptions are handled through Google Play. To determine whether a course should be unlocked, the app exchanges purchase-related information with Google Play Billing, such as product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase-verification information made available by Google Play.

The app does not collect or store your complete payment card details. Google processes payments and relevant transaction information according to its own terms and privacy policy. Redemption codes are submitted through Google Play; entering a code in the app does not itself grant access.

3. Speech synthesis

The app uses the text-to-speech engine available on your device to pronounce Mandarin Chinese text. It does not use the microphone for this feature. Depending on the speech engine you choose and its settings, text sent for speech synthesis may be processed by that engine's provider, including through an online service. The provider's privacy terms apply to that processing.

4. Data sharing and external services

The app does not sell personal information. It does not operate a separate developer-hosted account database for the educational features described above. Information may be processed by the following third parties or platform services where required to provide functionality:

  • Google Play: purchases, promotional-code redemptions, purchase status, and entitlement restoration.
  • Your device's text-to-speech provider: speech synthesis when you request audio playback.
  • Android/Google device backup services: if device backup is enabled, local app settings may be included in backups subject to the operating system and your backup settings.

For more about Google's handling of information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Security

Locally saved settings and course-entitlement identifiers are stored using the Android app's local storage mechanisms and are subject to the device's security controls. Purchase operations use Google Play Billing. The app does not ask you to enter bank card credentials into an app-managed payment form.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored app information remains on the device until you change it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can remove this local information through your device's app settings. Device backups, if enabled, are managed separately under your Android/Google backup settings.

The app does not maintain a developer-hosted user account to delete. Google Play transaction records and information held by speech-engine or backup providers are governed by those providers' retention and deletion policies. Uninstalling the app does not cancel or erase Google Play purchase records.

7. Children and families

This app contains educational language and physics materials. It does not ask users to register personal profiles. Where children use the app, a parent or guardian should manage Google Play purchase settings and any applicable device or family controls.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the app's features or data-handling practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For questions about privacy or the handling of information in Mandarino Chinese for Physics, email mandarinohsk@tiscali.it.
